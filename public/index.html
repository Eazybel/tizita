FAQ
The INSA Talent Center is a program that identifies, trains and supports cyber security talent through workshops, bootcamps and mentorships.
Students and early-career professionals with an interest in cybersecurity are encouraged to apply. Specific campaigns may target particular age groups or skill levels.
Registration details are published with each cohort. Check the official INSA announcements or use the signup page on this site for current registration links.
Requirements vary by program. Common expectations include basic computer literacy and a strong interest in learning cybersecurity topics.
Fees depend on the specific initiative. Some training is free or sponsored, while other programs may charge a fee—refer to official announcements for each event.
Use the official INSA contact channels or check the registration page for contact information and support.
